Let patients book a doctor online. Real free times, reminder emails, a cancel link, and a bookings dashboard for your clinics. Free, no limits.


== Description ==

**DocBooker lets patients book an appointment with a doctor on your WordPress site, and gives your front desk one place to see and manage every booking.**

Patients pick a department, a doctor, a day and a free time, enter their details and get a confirmation email with a link to add the visit to their calendar or cancel it. The day before, they get a reminder. You see every booking on a dashboard, new ones highlighted until someone opens them.

It suits a single doctor, a family practice, a dental or eye clinic, a group of clinics or a hospital, and works with any theme. No coding.

= Set up in five minutes =

DocBooker makes your Doctors and Clinics pages when you activate it. A short setup wizard then asks what kind of practice you run, makes your booking page, and can add sample doctors, clinics and departments so you can see everything working straight away. Sample data comes out again with one click.

= What patients get =

* **A booking form that walks them through it** — Department → Doctor → Day and time → Details → Done. Place it on any page with `[wpddb_doctor_booking_form]`.
* **Only times that are really free** — the calendar shows the doctor's working days, hides holidays and past times, and never offers a time that is already booked.
* **A booking box on every doctor's page** — patients can book from the doctor's profile too.
* **Confirmation and reminder emails** — a confirmation straight away and a reminder the day before.
* **Add to calendar** — Google Calendar or Apple / Outlook, from the email.
* **Cancel from the email** — a safe link lets patients cancel up to the time you choose, so the slot goes to someone else.
* **The fee up front** — show each doctor's fee in your currency; patients pay at the clinic.

= What your clinic gets =

* **Dashboard** — today's appointments, the next 7 days, bookings per day, busiest doctors, bookings by department and fees to collect, for any period, clinic or doctor.
* **Bookings** — search by name, booking ID, email or phone; filter by date; open a booking to see the patient, the appointment and its history; call, email, cancel or delete.
* **New bookings stand out** — online bookings stay highlighted until someone opens them, and the menu shows how many are new.
* **Add bookings yourself** — for phone and walk-in patients, with the doctor's free times shown.
* **Doctors** — photo, job title, speciality, degrees, workplace, email, fee and a weekly schedule per clinic, with quick "fill times" and "copy to other days".
* **Clinics** — unlimited clinics with address, phone, email and a map.
* **Holidays** — block a doctor at a clinic for a range of dates.
* **Booking rules** — how far ahead patients can book, how much notice you need, and how late they can cancel.
* **Doctor emails** — doctors get their own booking, cancel and reminder emails.
* **Booking Manager role** — let front-desk staff manage bookings without full admin access.
* **Settings in one place** — every setting on one screen with search, grouped by what it changes.

= What your website gets =

* **Doctor and clinic pages** — ready-made list and profile pages with two card designs: a portrait card and a directory row.
* **Doctor filter widget** — visitors filter doctors by department and clinic.
* **Shortcodes** — show any set of doctors `[wpddbdoctor id="…"]` or clinics `[wpddbclinic id="…"]` anywhere.
* **Your look** — main, second and border colours, card backgrounds and text colours, or leave any of them on "None" to keep your theme's look.
* **Your web addresses** — choose the words used in doctor, department and clinic links.
* **Works with your theme** — tested with Astra, Blocksy, GeneratePress, Kadence, Neve and Storefront.
* **Follows your site** — dates and times use your WordPress time zone and date and time formats.
* **Translation-ready** — text domain `doc-booker`.

== Installation ==

1. In your WordPress admin go to **Plugins → Add New**, search for **DocBooker**, then click **Install Now** and **Activate**.
2. The setup wizard opens. Choose your kind of practice, and add sample data if you want to try things first.
3. Go to **DocBooker → All Doctors** to add your doctors with their clinics and weekly schedules.
4. Share your booking page. You can also place the booking form on any page with `[wpddb_doctor_booking_form]`.
5. New bookings appear in **DocBooker → Bookings**. Settings are under **DocBooker → Dashboard**, in the menu on the left.


== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is DocBooker really free? =
Yes. Booking, reminders, the cancel link, the dashboard, unlimited doctors and unlimited clinics are all free. DocBooker Pro adds online payment, clinic staff accounts, billing, lab tests, a patient portal, blocks and site designs.

= How do patients book? =
They open your booking page (or any doctor's page), choose a department, a doctor, a day and a free time, enter their name, phone and email, and confirm. They get a confirmation email straight away.

= Can I have more than one clinic? =
Yes, as many as you like, in the free version. Each doctor can work at several clinics on different days. Pro adds clinic manager and front-desk accounts that only see their own clinic.

= Do patients get reminders? =
Yes. A reminder email goes out about a day before the appointment. You can switch reminders off for patients or doctors.

= Can patients cancel? =
Yes, from the link in their email, up to the number of hours before the appointment that you choose. You can also turn this off.

= Can I take payment online? =
Online payment with Stripe or Razorpay is in DocBooker Pro. In the free version the fee is shown and patients pay at the clinic.

= Does it work with my theme? =
DocBooker works with any well-made theme and is tested with Astra, Blocksy, GeneratePress, Kadence, Neve and Storefront.

= Emails don't arrive. What should I do? =
Many hosts don't send WordPress emails reliably. Install an SMTP plugin (for example WP Mail SMTP or FluentSMTP) and connect it to your email provider.

= Is it translation-ready? =
Yes. DocBooker ships a `.pot` file (text domain `doc-booker`).

= Where do I get help? =
Ask in the [DocBooker support forum](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/doc-booker/). We answer every question there, and the answers help other clinics too.
